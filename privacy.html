Privacy Policy

Pokelike is a free, fan-made browser game. We keep things simple.

Data we store

Your game progress — current run, Pokédex, achievements and settings — is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. It never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Third-party content

Pokémon sprites and data are loaded from the open PokeAPI project. The interface font is served by Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Cookies & tracking

This build uses no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.

Clearing your data

You can erase all stored progress at any time from Settings → “Erase all data”, or by clearing your browser's site data.
